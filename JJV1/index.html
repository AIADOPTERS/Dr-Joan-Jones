About the show

A place for anyone who has been told their best days are behind them.

About Dr. Joan

The woman behind
the wisdom.

Dr. Joan Jones is an experienced psychotherapist, counselor, and faith-centered teacher and mentor, approaching her 67th birthday. She holds multiple degrees and has built a meaningful career — and she still practices today because she loves serving others and sharing the wisdom she has gained.

She has a warm Jamaican accent, a captivating voice, a direct style, and a strong sense of humor — with a natural gift for recognizing potential in people and helping them see possibilities they don't yet see for themselves.

JJ
Portrait placeholder

Her story

Hard seasons, and what she found in them.

Her own path has included divorce, family challenges, and caregiving for an adult son with chronic health concerns. She has wrestled with questions of faith, and worked through self-doubt and imposter syndrome to finally recognize the value of her own voice and life experience.

After a full career, she returned to school in her 50s and earned her Ph.D. over 60 — proof, in her own life, of the very thing this podcast is about: you are not finished. That belief is what she now brings to every listener.

Why the podcast

Wisdom is meant to be shared.

Dr. Joan is building this podcast and platform to share her wisdom, faith, professional insight, and personal story — giving listeners hope, language for what they're going through, and permission to believe there's still more available to them.
